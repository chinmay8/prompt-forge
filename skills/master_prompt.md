# Skill: Master Prompt Framework

## Core Principle
Anchor visual generation in emotional tone and recognizable visual/cinematic references first, then ground the subject with composition, lighting, and camera settings.

## Mandatory Output Formula
Synthesize the user's request into a cohesive, descriptive prompt following this exact structural flow:
`[Emotional tone] + [Visual reference] + [Subject & Action] + [Composition & Environment] + [Lighting] + [Camera settings & Film/Render Style]`

## Mix & Match Visual Building Blocks
Draw from these combinations to enrich sparse meta-prompts:
- **Emotional Tone:** Intense, mysterious, dark suspense, warm and inviting, serene and meditative, epic blockbuster energy, nostalgic, surreal and dreamlike, sleek minimalist luxury, high-energy urban grit.
- **Visual References:** Iconic film aesthetics (e.g., *Blade Runner 2049*, *Dune*, *The Dark Knight*, Studio Ghibli, Wes Anderson symmetry), commercial styles (Apple product commercial, architectural digest editorial), or medium styles (1980s retro film, watercolor, Unreal Engine 5).
- **Composition & Camera:** Rule-of-thirds framing, symmetrical wide shot, dynamic Dutch angle, macro close-up, aerial drone perspective, 35mm or 85mm prime lens, shallow depth of field, Cinestill 800T or Kodak Portra film grain.
- **Lighting:** Chiaroscuro contrast, neon reflections on wet asphalt, golden hour backlight, volumetric god rays, soft diffused studio lighting.

## Strict Execution Rules
1. Blend the 6 elements into natural, evocative descriptive prose (do not use `+` signs or bracketed headers in the final output).
2. Ensure the emotional tone and visual reference appear at the very beginning to set the latent aesthetic immediately.
3. Output **ONLY** the final engineered prompt—no preamble, bullet points, or commentary.

## Example Output
Intense, mysterious, dark suspense visual inspired by The Dark Knight, Dune, and Blade Runner 2049, featuring a customized Mumbai auto-rickshaw navigating an interstellar asteroid highway with a cybernetic female passenger, framed in a dynamic low-angle wide composition amidst swirling cosmic dust and steampunk brass docking rings, dramatic volumetric starlight and neon thruster rim lighting, shot on IMAX film with an anamorphic lens and rich Cinestill 800T color grading.
